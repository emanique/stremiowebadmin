# v0.1.3-rc.8 — HLS IPTV playback and FFmpeg lifecycle

Candidate validation build, not the stable release.

- Registers external HTTP/IPTV HLS sessions even without a torrent infohash.
- Preserves job and workload correlation, so segments refresh the playback registry.
- GC reaping closes HLS registry entries when the last encoder workload ends.
- Keeps the previous RC7 monitor fix (unregistered FFmpeg processes are visible, not mistaken for verified playback).
- Does not change streaming paths, codecs, or the user-configured VPN routing.

Verification: external master-playlist regression and GC tests; full-suite results must be reviewed before promotion. Operational fresh-install and long-duration IPTV validation remain required to promote this candidate to stable.
