# v0.1.3-rc.7 — FFmpeg monitor correlation

Release candidate (not stable). Fixes hidden FFmpeg processes on WebAdmin when HLS playback registry has no matching active workload. Processes are marked UNREGISTERED when the registry is empty or lacks the job. A remux using copy video and audio codecs is reported as Direct Stream, not hardware transcoding.

Does not alter the streaming-server core, VPN traffic routing, or HLS garbage collection. Known follow-up: investigate why FFmpeg processes can outlive the playback registry's active session window. Source-build packages do not bundle images.
